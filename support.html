Where are my photos stored?

Inside the app, on your phone. The app never uploads them.

How do I move to a new phone?

An iCloud or computer backup restores the app with its photos. You can also use Export all originals in the app, which gives you a zip of every photo, free, at any time.

My eyes were not found

In Library, open the photo, tap Fix eye position, and drag the two rings onto your pupils. This helps with sunglasses and odd angles.

How do I import old selfies?

Use Import and pick photos from Photos or Files. Each one is placed on the day it was taken, read from the photo's own metadata.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Subscriptions are managed in your Apple account settings (Settings, your name, Subscriptions). Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I restore a purchase?

Use the Restore Purchases button on the Pro screen while signed in with the same Apple account.

What does lighting evening do?

It works like a compressor in music. Small day to day differences are left alone, big ones are pulled partway toward your usual look, and a hard limit means no photo is ever blown out. Slow real changes like a tan or a haircut are kept, because each day is compared with the weeks around it. It is a Pro feature.

What is the camera ghost, and how do I change it?

It is a faint copy of your first photo laid over the live camera, with a crossbar, to help you line up your eyes. Use the slider to make it stronger or fainter. To use a different photo as the ghost, pick one in the Library.
