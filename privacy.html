Privacy Policy

Dayface by Chris Dunn. Dated November 10, 2026.

Short version: Dayface keeps your photos on your phone. I do not collect, see, sell or share any of your data.

What the app stores, and where

Your daily photos, their dates, and your settings are stored inside the app on your device. The app never uploads them. Finding your eyes uses Apple's on-device Vision framework, so no photo is sent anywhere for processing.

Camera and photo library

The camera permission is used to take your daily photo. Photo library access is used only when you choose to import existing selfies, or to save an exported video to Photos. Files access works the same way: only what you pick.

Notifications

Daily reminders are optional and off unless you turn them on. They are scheduled on your device and are not sent from any server.

No account, no analytics, no third parties

There is no account or sign in. The app has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and contains no third party code or services.

iCloud and device backup

If you have iCloud Backup turned on for your phone, the app's data, including your photos, is part of that backup like any other app's data. That backup is between you and Apple. I cannot see it.

Purchases

Dayface Pro purchases are handled by Apple. I receive no payment details and no personal information, only anonymous sales reports from Apple.

Children

Dayface is not directed at children under 13. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete photos inside the app, or delete the app, and the photos stored in it are removed from your device. You can also export all your original photos as a zip first. Copies in your own device backups follow your backup settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
