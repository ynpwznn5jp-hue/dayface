Dayface

One photo of your face a day, played back as a time-lapse with your eyes locked in place.

  • A faint ghost of your first photo and a crossbar line up the camera.
  • Your eyes are found on the phone and pinned to the same spot in every frame.
  • Lighting is evened out so day to day swings do not flicker.
  • Play 3 to 30 photos a second, or export a video for Reels, TikTok and Stories.
  • Import selfies you already have, each on the day it was taken.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. Photos stay on your phone.
